## What I do

Signature Canvas Makers builds custom boat canvas in Hampton, VA. Every boat is different, so every piece is custom. The shop scans each boat in 3D, and I turn that scan into something the shop floor can build:

1. **Model the canvas on the scan.** In Rhino I build the cover or enclosure surface directly on the scanned boat geometry.
2. **Break it into flat panels.** I split the 3D surface into numbered panels that can be cut from flat fabric and sewn back into the right 3D shape.
3. **Generate cut files.** I prepare the flat patterns as cut files for an Eastman automated cutting table.
4. **Write build sheets.** Each job gets a build sheet so fabricators know how the panels go together.
