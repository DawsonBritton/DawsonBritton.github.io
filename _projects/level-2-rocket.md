## Goal

Level 2 certification allows J, K, and L motors. I'm designing this rocket to earn L2 and to be the flight test bed for my [flight computer]({{ '/projects/flight-computer/' | relative_url }}). It uses dual deployment: a small drogue at apogee limits drift, and the main opens low.

## Design

I designed the rocket in OpenRocket and simulated it across the motor range it's meant to fly on: a mild J350 for low, close-in flights, and an L1090 for a high-performance flight.
